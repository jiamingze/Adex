indigo ink, distant misty mountains, subtle sparse ivory stars and a gently suggested

minimalist app identity, generous negative space, NOT busy fantasy art, NOT cartoon,
no glowing technological effects. The upper and lower edges fade gracefully to warm
ivory paper, so it blends with a white app screen. Keep the figure and essential scene
within the central 60% width for adaptive cropping. At upper center elegant restrained
typography with exact brand 'Adex'; below brand in small beautifully legible Chinese
text exactly '让智能，成为日常。'. Avoid other text, seals, watermarks, borders,
UI mockups or device frames. Actual usable full-bleed launch artwork, not a screenshot.
