# Community guidelines / 社区规范

Adex welcomes contributors of different experience levels and backgrounds. English
and Chinese are both welcome. Ask questions, explain tradeoffs and criticize ideas
constructively; give newcomers time to learn.

欢迎不同经验和背景的贡献者，支持中文与英文。请尊重提问、解释取舍，针对代码和设计讨论，帮助新朋友学习。

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, impersonation, doxxing and posting
another person's private information are not acceptable. Do not spam issues or use
project spaces for unsolicited promotion. Never post credentials or personal logs.

不接受骚扰、歧视、威胁、冒充、泄露隐私或垃圾推广。不要公开凭据或包含个人信息的日志。

Maintainers may edit/remove inappropriate content, close disruptive discussions and
restrict participation. Explain moderation decisions when doing so does not disclose
private information. Apply these expectations consistently to all participants.

维护者可移除不当内容、关闭破坏性讨论或限制参与，在不泄露隐私的前提下说明处理原因，并一致执行。

For a conduct concern, open a minimal issue requesting a private maintainer contact
without publishing names or sensitive details, or use an existing private contact
channel with the maintainer. For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
No confidential reporting channel or response SLA is guaranteed by this file.

如需反馈社区行为问题，可使用已有私下渠道，或开一个仅请求私下联系方式的简短 Issue；不要披露隐私或当事人信息。
安全漏洞请遵循 SECURITY.md。本文件不承诺专用保密渠道或响应时限。
